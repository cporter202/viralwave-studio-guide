# Getting Started: Website In, Content Out

This is the full onboarding flow — what happens from the moment you land on [viralwavestudio.com](https://viralwavestudio.com) to your first month running on autopilot.

## Step 1 — Paste your website

On the homepage, enter your business website URL and submit. ViralWave's crawler reads your site — your services, your offers, your tone, your imagery — and builds a **brand profile** from it automatically.

What it reads: page text, titles, and descriptions from your homepage plus a few key pages. What it doesn't do: download your photos, run scripts, or crawl competitors. If your site is thin on text, you'll get a chance to fill in the gaps manually.

No website? You can describe your business by hand instead — it works, it's just a few more minutes.

## Step 2 — Get 4 finished posts free

Within minutes, ViralWave generates **four complete posts** for your business: captions plus AI-generated images, built from your brand profile.

These aren't samples or templates. They're real, post-ready content. This is the moment most people get it — the posts sound like their business because they were built from their business.

## Step 3 — Create your account

Google or email sign-in. Email signups verify before generation starts, and your website and preview carry over through verification — you won't lose your place.

## Step 4 — Tune your brand voice

A short questionnaire walks you through the choices that shape your content: your role, your posting cadence, which platforms you care about, tone, emoji style, audience, and goals. One question at a time, illustrated choices, about two minutes.

You can also **upload photos** — of you, your team, your work, your products. These feed the image generation and the face-reference feature, so your visuals actually look like your business instead of stock.

More on this: [03 — Brand voice](03-brand-voice.md)

## Step 5 — Pick a plan

[Starter ($15/mo), Pro ($29/mo), or Powerhouse ($49/mo)](07-plans-and-pricing.md). Your plan unlocks the rest of your month's content — around 50 posts total, a balanced mix across content types.

## Step 6 — Review your month

Your full month appears in a review screen built for speed: one post at a time, swipe-style. For each post you can:

- **Approve** — it's in the schedule
- **Skip** — it's out, no hard feelings
- **Edit** — tweak the caption yourself
- **Ask AI to revise** — "make it funnier," "shorter," "more professional"

Approving never publishes anything by itself. Nothing goes live until you say so.

Full walkthrough: [04 — Review workflow](04-review-workflow.md)

## Step 7 — Connect your platforms and go autopilot

Connect your social accounts, enable publishing, and your approved month goes out on schedule — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, and YouTube.

From here, your monthly routine is: review the new month's posts (about 10 minutes), approve, done. The daily grind is gone.
