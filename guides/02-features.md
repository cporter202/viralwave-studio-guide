# Features: Everything ViralWave Studio Does

## AI post generator

The core of the platform. From your brand profile, ViralWave generates a full month of social posts — captions, hooks, and creative direction — balanced across the content mix that actually grows accounts: educational posts, behind-the-scenes, social proof, personal story, and promotional content.

You're never writing from a blank page again. You're editing and approving.

## AI image generation

Every post gets visuals. ViralWave generates on-brand images matched to your visual identity — colors, style, and subject matter drawn from your brand profile and your uploaded photos. No stock-photo look, no generic filler.

## Video lab

Short-form video is where the reach is, and it's the most time-consuming format to produce. The video lab generates AI video content sized for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts — so your month includes motion, not just stills.

## Blog generator

SEO-structured blog posts written in your brand voice. These pull double duty: they rank and drive search traffic, and they feed your social content — one blog post becomes a week of social posts through the repurposing flow.

## Face-reference brand authority

Upload your photo once, and ViralWave can feature your likeness consistently across generated visuals — same person, on-brand every time. For founders and creators, this is the difference between "AI content" and "my content." Your face builds trust; now it can show up in every post without a photoshoot.

## Smart scheduler

Your approved month, queued across all eight platforms from one place. Set it once per month — the calendar fills itself from your approved posts.

## Analytics with AI insights

Performance tracking across your connected platforms with AI-powered analysis of what's working. Instead of building spreadsheets, you read a summary: which posts landed, which flopped, and what to do more of next month.

## Marketing assistant

A built-in AI chatbot for content strategy questions. "What should I post about our new service?" "How do I handle negative comments?" It's the strategy layer on top of the generation engine.

## RSS-to-posts

Connect a blog or industry RSS feed and ViralWave turns new entries into social posts automatically. Your content engine keeps running even when you're not thinking about it.

## Content library

Every generated post, every decision, saved to your personal content library. Revisit past months, reuse winners, and keep a searchable archive of everything the platform has made for you.

## AI agent integrations (MCP)

Connect Codex, ChatGPT, or Claude directly to your ViralWave account over a secure MCP connection. Ask your assistant to summarize your brand voice, review drafts, check scheduled posts, or generate new ones — from inside the AI tool you already use.
