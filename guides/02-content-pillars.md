# Content Pillars: Never Stare at a Blank Page Again

Content pillars are the 4–5 recurring themes your content rotates through. They turn "what should I post today?" into "which pillar is today?" — a much easier question.

## Why pillars work

- **Decisions get cheap.** Five choices instead of infinite ones.
- **Your feed gets rhythm.** Followers learn what to expect and when.
- **Batching gets fast.** Prompts are written per pillar, so generation is mechanical.
- **Your brand gets clear.** After a month, a stranger can describe what your business is about from your feed alone.

## The 5 universal pillars

Almost every business can run on these five. Adjust the names to fit your voice:

### 1. Teach (educate)
Tips, how-tos, mistakes to avoid, industry explainers. Positions you as the expert. This is your highest-value pillar — save and share magnets.

*Example: A bakery posts "3 reasons your sourdough is dense (and the 30-second fix)."*

### 2. Show (behind the scenes)
Your process, your workspace, your team, your day. Builds trust because it's real. Lowest production effort — phone photos win here.

*Example: A plumber posts a 20-second clip snaking a drain with the caption "Tuesday."*

### 3. Prove (social proof)
Reviews, testimonials, before/afters, results, case studies. Answers the prospect's real question: "does this work for people like me?"

*Example: A fitness coach posts a client's 90-day transformation with their permission.*

### 4. Sell (offers)
Promotions, new products, seasonal offers, booking reminders, urgency. Keep this to ~20% of your content. Nobody follows an ad channel.

*Example: A salon posts "Two cancellation slots opened for Saturday — DM to grab one."*

### 5. Human (story & values)
Your why, your opinions, your community involvement, your failures. This is what turns followers into fans. People buy from people.

*Example: A restaurant owner posts about sponsoring the local Little League team.*

## Pillar mixes by business type

| Business | Teach | Show | Prove | Sell | Human |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local service (plumber, electrician) | DIY tips, what pros check | Job site clips | Before/after, reviews | Seasonal tune-up offers | Crew stories, community |
| Restaurant / café | Recipes, ingredient sourcing | Kitchen prep, new dishes | Customer reviews, busy nights | Specials, events | Staff spotlights, local ties |
| E-commerce / product | How to use it, care guides | Packing orders, warehouse | Unboxings, reviews | Drops, sales, bundles | Founder story, mission |
| Coach / consultant | Frameworks, mistakes | Client session clips (with permission) | Case studies, wins | Program enrollment | Personal journey, opinions |
| Real estate agent | Market explainers, buying tips | Listing walkthroughs | Sold stories, testimonials | New listings, open houses | Neighborhood love, personal |
| SaaS / creator | Tutorials, workflows | Building in public | User wins, metrics | Launches, pricing | Roadmap, lessons learned |

## Building your pillar rotation

A 30-day month at one post per day, five pillars:

- **Week pattern:** Teach → Show → Prove → Human → Teach → Show → Sell → (repeat)

That gives you roughly: Teach 8, Show 8, Prove 4, Human 6, Sell 4. Selling stays at ~13%. Teaching and showing — your trust builders — carry the month.

The [`generate-calendar.py`](../scripts/generate-calendar.py) script builds this rotation for you automatically. Feed it your pillars and it outputs a dated 30-day CSV with pillar assignments.

## The pillar test

Before you lock your pillars, run each one through this:

1. Can I make **6+ posts a month** in this pillar without repeating myself?
2. Does it serve **the audience I want** (not just the audience I have)?
3. Does at least one pillar **directly drive revenue** (Sell or Prove)?

If a pillar fails test 1, merge it into another. Five strong pillars beat seven thin ones.
