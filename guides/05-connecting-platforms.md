# Connecting Platforms: Where Your Content Goes

ViralWave Studio publishes across eight platforms. This guide covers connecting them and what to expect from each.

## The eight platforms

| Platform | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | Local businesses, 30+ audiences, community | Still the highest-ROI platform for most local service businesses |
| **Instagram** | Visual brands, creators, under-40 audiences | Reels for reach, carousels for saves, stories for loyalty |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, consultants, founders, professional authority | Founder personal posts outperform company pages — connect yours |
| **X (Twitter)** | Real-time conversation, tech/business audiences | Threads for depth, singles for presence |
| **Threads** | Casual, conversational presence | Low-production text posts do well here |
| **Pinterest** | Evergreen traffic, e-commerce, visual niches | Content lives for months — the slow-burn traffic engine |
| **TikTok** | Discovery, under-35 audiences, raw authenticity | Raw beats polished; your behind-the-scenes content shines here |
| **YouTube** | Long-form authority + Shorts discovery | Shorts feed the algorithm, long-form builds depth |

## Connecting an account

From your dashboard, open the connections or integrations area, choose a platform, and authorize via OAuth. You log in to the platform itself — ViralWave never sees or stores your password.

You control two things per platform:

1. **Connected or not** — only connected platforms receive content
2. **Publishing enabled or not** — connected for analytics vs. actually posting

This separation matters: you can connect everything for analytics while only auto-publishing to the two or three platforms you care about most.

## Recommended setup

**Start with 2 primary platforms** — the ones where your buyers actually are. Enable publishing there.

**Add the rest gradually.** Connect the others for analytics first. When you see a platform earning its keep in the analytics, flip on publishing.

**Founder-led beats brand-led on LinkedIn.** If you're a consultant, coach, or agency owner, connect your personal LinkedIn profile, not just the company page. Personal reach is a multiple of page reach.

## Platform-specific tips

- **Instagram/Facebook** — Reels and carousels get priority in the generated mix. Stories are scheduled between main posts to keep you top-of-feed.
- **TikTok** — the video lab output is sized and structured for TikTok natively. Keep your raw phone clips coming too; the algorithm rewards authenticity.
- **LinkedIn** — text posts and document-style carousels are the current reach winners for professional content.
- **Pinterest** — pins get keyword-rich titles and descriptions automatically, since Pinterest is a search engine wearing a social network's clothes.
- **YouTube** — Shorts go out on the discovery schedule; longer content is batched for your main upload cadence.

## Disconnecting

Revoke anytime from the connections area. Scheduled-but-unpublished posts for a disconnected platform simply won't go out — nothing posts to an account you haven't authorized.
