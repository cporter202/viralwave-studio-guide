# Review Workflow: The 10-Minute Monthly Approval

Nothing publishes without your approval. This guide covers the review screen — the one monthly task that keeps you in control while the autopilot does the work.

## How review works

After ViralWave generates your month (around 50 posts), they land in a review screen designed for speed: **one post at a time**, large and readable, with the image and caption together. It feels like swiping through photos, not managing a spreadsheet.

For each post, you have four moves:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The post joins your scheduled month |
| **Skip** | The post is out — no penalty, no clutter |
| **Edit** | Tweak the caption yourself, inline |
| **AI revise** | Tell the AI what to change ("funnier," "shorter," "more professional") and it regenerates |

You can also browse the full collection, search it, and filter by content type. Changed your mind? Undo is there — decisions aren't permanent until publishing runs.

## Approval never publishes

This is the important part: **approving a post does not schedule or publish it.** Approval is curation. Publishing is a separate step that only happens for your approved month, on accounts you've connected, with publishing enabled. You're always the gatekeeper.

## The 10-minute routine

Most users settle into this rhythm:

1. **Skim the month** (3 min) — swipe through, get a feel for the mix
2. **Kill the misses** (2 min) — skip anything that doesn't feel like you
3. **Fix the near-misses** (3 min) — quick edits or AI revisions on posts that are 80% there
4. **Approve the rest** (2 min) — bulk-approve what's left

Ten minutes, once a month. That's the entire "content job" once you're set up.

## Working with AI revisions

The revise function is where the magic compounds. Be specific:

- Weak: "make it better"
- Strong: "cut it in half, lead with the tip, drop the hashtags"
- Strong: "more conversational — like I'm talking to a customer across the counter"
- Strong: "this is too salesy for a Tuesday post, make it educational"

Each revision teaches the system your taste. By month three, you'll revise less because the first drafts land closer.

## What to look for

- **Voice check** — does it sound like you? If three posts in a row feel off, revisit [brand voice settings](03-brand-voice.md)
- **Offer accuracy** — prices, dates, and details should match reality. The AI works from your website; if your site is outdated, flag it
- **Image fit** — does the visual match the caption? Regenerate the image if it's off
- **Mix balance** — roughly 80% value (teach, show, prove, story) to 20% promotional is the healthy ratio
