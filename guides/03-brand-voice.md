# Brand Voice: How ViralWave Learns to Sound Like You

Generic AI content is the fastest way to get ignored. ViralWave's brand voice system exists to solve exactly that — your posts should read like you wrote them on your best day, not like a robot wrote them on an average one.

## Layer 1 — Your website

The foundation. When you onboard with a website URL, ViralWave reads your actual copy — how you describe your services, the words you choose, the tone of your about page. A playful bakery and a straight-talking law firm produce very different content from the same engine, because they start from very different source material.

This is why the website onboarding matters: it's the difference between "AI content" and "my content, generated."

## Layer 2 — The questionnaire

After your free preview, a short guided questionnaire tunes the voice:

- **Your role** — owner, marketer, creator, agency
- **Posting cadence** — how often you want to show up
- **Platforms** — where your audience actually is
- **Tone** — professional, friendly, bold, playful, or your own mix
- **Emoji style** — heavy, light, or none
- **Audience** — who you're talking to, in plain words
- **Goals** — bookings, sales, followers, authority, or all of the above

One question at a time, about two minutes total. Your answers shape every post from that point on.

## Layer 3 — Your photos

Upload photos of yourself, your team, your workspace, your products, your work. These do two jobs:

1. **Visual reference** — generated images match your real business instead of generic stock
2. **Face reference** — the brand-authority feature keeps your likeness consistent across generated visuals

The more real material you give it, the less "AI" the output looks.

## Tuning it over time

Brand voice isn't set-and-forget. Two feedback loops keep it sharp:

**The review loop.** Every time you edit a caption or ask the AI to revise ("shorter," "less formal," "more like my about page"), you're teaching it. Edits compound — month three sounds more like you than month one.

**The analytics loop.** The AI insights flag which voice patterns perform best with your audience. If your casual posts outperform your professional ones 3-to-1, you'll know — and the next month leans in.

## Tips for the best results

- **Write your website like you talk.** The crawler can only learn from what's there. If your site is corporate-speak, your first drafts will be too — fix the source or lean on the questionnaire.
- **Upload at least 5 photos.** Variety (you, team, workspace, product, work-in-progress) gives the image engine real material.
- **Edit the first month actively.** The revision requests you make in month one are the highest-leverage training the system gets.
- **Be specific in revision requests.** "Make it sound more like me" is weak. "Shorter sentences, more direct, drop the exclamation marks" is strong.
