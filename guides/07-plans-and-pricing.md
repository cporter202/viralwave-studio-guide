**Pick Starter if:** you're a solo operator or local business that needs to show up consistently on 2–3 platforms. It's the full autopilot at the lowest entry point.

**Pick Pro if:** you're growing, posting across most platforms, and want more monthly volume — more posts, more images, more video. This is the sweet spot for creators, coaches, and scaling local brands.

**Pick Powerhouse if:** content is a core growth channel for you — agencies managing clients, e-commerce brands running constant promotions, or anyone who wants maximum output with room to spare.
