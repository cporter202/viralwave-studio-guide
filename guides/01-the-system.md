# The 5-Step Content Autopilot System

This is the complete system this repo is built around. Read this first — everything else is a deep dive into one of these steps.

## The problem

Most businesses approach content like this: wake up, think "I should post something," stare at a blank screen, post something rushed at 9pm, repeat until burnout. Then three weeks of silence. Then guilt. Then the cycle restarts.

The problem was never creativity. It was the **daily decision**. Every day you're deciding *what* to post, *when* to post, *where* to post, and *how* to make it. That's four decisions a day, 120 a month, made on top of actually running your business. Of course it collapses.

## The fix: batch, don't dribble

The autopilot system replaces 120 daily decisions with **one monthly session**. Here's the machine:

### Step 1 — Pillars (30 minutes, once)

Define 4–5 content pillars: recurring themes that everything you post fits into. Pillars kill the blank page forever, because you're never inventing a topic from scratch — you're picking a pillar, then a prompt.

Full walkthrough: [02-content-pillars.md](02-content-pillars.md)

### Step 2 — Batch generation (2–3 hours, monthly)

In one sitting, generate every post for the month. Not with willpower — with the [prompt library](03-prompt-library.md). Each prompt is designed for a specific pillar and format. You feed it your pillar and a few business details, it gives you a finished draft, you edit and move on. Thirty posts, one afternoon.

The realistic math: an unassisted post takes ~45 minutes (ideate, write, find image, format, post). A batched, prompt-assisted post takes ~10. Thirty posts go from **22 hours to 5**.

### Step 3 — Visuals (1 hour, monthly)

Every post gets a visual. Text-only posts underperform image and video posts on every platform, every time. You have three options, in order of effort:

1. **AI-generated images** matched to your brand (fastest consistent look)
2. **Template-based graphics** (Canva templates with your brand kit)
3. **Raw phone photos/video** (highest trust, best for behind-the-scenes pillars)

Most people do a mix: AI or templated graphics for tips/offers, raw phone content for behind-the-scenes and story pillars.

### Step 4 — Schedule (30 minutes, monthly)

Queue everything at once. Pick your platforms and cadence from the [platform cheat sheet](../README.md#platform-cheat-sheet), then schedule the full month in one go. Detailed workflow: [04-scheduling-and-publishing.md](04-scheduling-and-publishing.md)

Once it's scheduled, you're done creating for the month. The only daily task left is 5 minutes of engagement (replies, comments) — which you can't automate and shouldn't.

### Step 5 — Review (20 minutes, weekly)

Once a week, check what landed. Save your top 3 posts. Note the pattern. Feed the winners back into next month's batch session as "more like this." That's the compounding loop — month two beats month one, month six beats month three.

Details: [05-measure-and-iterate.md](05-measure-and-iterate.md)

## The monthly rhythm

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Month start (one session) | Pillars check, batch 30 posts, pair visuals, schedule everything | 4–5 hours |
| Weekly (Friday?) | 20-minute review: top posts, saves, comments | 20 min |
| Daily | Engagement only: reply to comments, answer DMs | 5–10 min |

**Total: ~6 hours a month** for a daily presence across multiple platforms. That's the whole system.
