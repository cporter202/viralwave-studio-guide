## How it works

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it actually post for me, or do I have to hit publish?</strong></summary>

It posts for you — once you've approved your month, connected your accounts, and enabled publishing. Approval and publishing are separate steps, and you're the gatekeeper for both.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I edit what the AI writes?</strong></summary>

Every post. Approve, skip, edit inline, or ask the AI to revise it ("shorter," "funnier," "more professional"). Nothing goes live you haven't okayed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Will it sound like me or like a robot?</strong></summary>

Like you — if you set it up right. The brand profile starts from your website, the questionnaire tunes it, your photo uploads ground the visuals, and your edits compound over time. Month three sounds more like you than month one. See [03 — Brand voice](03-brand-voice.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What if my website is outdated?</strong></summary>

The AI learns from what's there, so outdated sites produce outdated details (old prices, old services). Either update the key pages first or correct details during review — and flag them in revision requests.
</details>

<summary><strong>Is this going to make me go viral?</strong></summary>
