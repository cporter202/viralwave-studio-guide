# Measure & Iterate: The 20-Minute Weekly Review

Month one of the system is a guess — an educated one, but a guess. The weekly review is what turns it into a machine that gets smarter every month. Twenty minutes, once a week.

## The only 5 numbers that matter

Ignore vanity dashboards. Track these five:

1. **Reach per post** — how many people saw it (top of funnel)
2. **Saves + shares per post** — the strongest quality signal on every platform
3. **Profile visits / follows from content** — is content converting to audience?
4. **Link clicks / DMs / inquiries** — is content converting to business?
5. **Your top 3 posts of the week** — the actual posts, saved, not just numbers

That's it. If a metric isn't on this list, it's not driving a decision.

## The 20-minute Friday review

**Minutes 0–5: Pull the numbers.**
Open each platform's native analytics. Write down reach, saves/shares, and follows for the week's posts. A simple spreadsheet beats a fancy dashboard.

**Minutes 5–12: Find the pattern.**
Look at your top 3 posts. Ask: what pillar were they? What format? What hook style? What topic? Write one sentence: "This week, [format] posts in the [pillar] pillar about [topic angle] won."

**Minutes 12–17: Find the dud.**
Your worst post of the week — why did it flop? Wrong pillar for the audience? Weak hook? Bad posting time? One sentence. No self-flagellation; it's data.

**Minutes 17–20: Feed next month.**
Add two lines to a running "winners" doc:
- MORE LIKE THIS: [the pattern that won]
- NEVER AGAIN: [the pattern that flopped]

Bring this doc to your next monthly batch session. It's the entire difference between month one and month six.

## When to change pillars

Don't touch your pillars for the first 60 days — you need data, not vibes. After that:

- A pillar that never cracks your top 10 posts after 60 days → **replace it**
- A format your audience saves 3x more than average → **double its share**
- A topic that drives DMs → **build a whole pillar around it**

Evolve, don't restart. The system compounds; reboots reset the compounding.

## The 90-day trajectory

- **Days 1–30:** You're learning. Post quality is uneven. That's fine — consistency is the win.
- **Days 31–60:** Patterns emerge. You know 2–3 things your audience loves. Lean in.
- **Days 61–90:** The machine hums. Batch sessions get faster, winners get clearer, content starts driving real inquiries.

Most people quit at day 25. The review ritual is what gets you to day 90.
