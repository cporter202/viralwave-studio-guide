# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this guide accurate. PRs are welcome.

## What we're looking for

- **Corrections** — outdated screenshots descriptions, changed flows, new features, pricing updates. Accuracy is the top priority for a product guide.
- **New examples** — agent prompts, workflow tips, platform-specific advice that you've tested.
- **Clarity fixes** — typos, broken links, confusing sections.

## Ground rules

1. This repo documents [ViralWave Studio](https://viralwavestudio.com) as it actually works. Don't document features that don't exist or guess at roadmap items.
2. Keep the voice direct and jargon-free, matching the existing guides.
3. Keep the CTAs pointing at viralwavestudio.com — this repo is the product's public guide and funnel.
4. Pricing changes: verify against the live site before submitting.

## How to submit

1. Fork the repo
2. Make your change on a branch
3. Open a PR with a one-paragraph description of what you changed and why

Small, accurate PRs get merged fast.
