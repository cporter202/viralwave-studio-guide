# Agent Prompts: Things to Ask Your Connected AI Assistant

Once you've connected Codex, ChatGPT, or Claude to ViralWave via MCP (see [06 — AI agents & MCP](../guides/06-ai-agents-mcp.md)), these prompts get you started. Copy, paste, adapt.

## Account check-in

```text
Use ViralWave to read my account context and summarize my brand voice
and connected platforms. Do not create or schedule anything.
```

## Weekly review

```text
Show me my scheduled posts for next week. For each one, give me the
caption's hook and flag anything that feels off-brand.
```

```text
Which of my ViralWave posts performed best in the last 30 days?
What pattern do you see in the winners?
```

## Drafting

```text
Generate 5 ViralWave draft posts about [topic/offer]. Save them as
drafts — do not schedule anything.
```

```text
I need 3 educational posts for next week about [topic]. Generate
drafts in my brand voice and save them.
```

## Scheduling (always confirms first)

```text
Schedule my approved draft about [topic] for Tuesday at 9am on
Instagram and Facebook. Confirm the exact post and time with me first.
```

## Video

```text
Generate a short-form video for my [product/offer] using ViralWave.
Check its status and let me know when it's ready.
```

## Content mix

```text
Give me a ViralWave content summary for this month: how many posts
per content type, and am I heavy or light on promotional content?
```
